I'm **Sahil Shaikh**, a software engineer with 1.5+ years of experience building backend systems, CRM software, mobile applications, and real-time applications. I work mostly with Node.js, React, React Native, PHP, and SQL/NoSQL databases.

I'm currently a Full Stack Developer at **Trunetic Infotech**, where I designed a multi-role RBAC architecture with Redis-backed token rotation, built a real-time chat and tracking pipeline, and architected a modular transport backend with 25+ REST endpoints. Before that, I was a Software Engineer at **Aalzatech Software Solutions**, working on lead scoring and multi-tenant inventory management systems.

I hold a BSc from **Mumbai University** and have contributed to open source projects including the V8 engine, Appwrite, and LocalSend.
